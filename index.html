📖 How to Use on Android
  • Step 1
    Open in Chrome
    Open your GSRTC ticket URL on your mobile Chrome browser.
  • Step 2
    Share to App
    Tap Chrome's menu (⋮) ➔ Share ➔ Select GSRTC Ticket Fixer.
  • Step 3
    Save Clean PDF
    The app loads the responsive ticket. Tap Save PDF to download the complete A4 copy.
